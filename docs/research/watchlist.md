## Known Limitations

### Selection Bias in Watchlist

The watchlist determines what papers get reviewed. It is inherently biased by:

- **School of thought**: Polat/Sagi/Israeli group dominates Gabor patch training literature. Papers from other groups (Chinese, Japanese, Brazilian research labs) may be underrepresented.
- **Publication bias**: Negative-result papers (training didn't work) are less likely to be published. The watchlist can only track what exists.
- **Language bias**: English-language journals are overrepresented. Relevant work in Russian, Japanese, etc. may be missed.
- **Query bias**: Pre-built search queries use English terms and may not surface non-English work.

**Mitigation**: Periodically review the watchlist itself for coverage gaps. Not a task for every run, but worth a check every few months.
